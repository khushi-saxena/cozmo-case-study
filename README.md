# Cozmo case study

Takes a handheld iPhone capture of a property and produces a dimensioned floor
plan: per-room polygons with wall lengths and ceiling heights, rooms stitched
with adjacency, openings, damage regions, scope line items, and a confidence
interval on every measurement. Photo, video and LiDAR inputs all run through
the same pipeline.

Khushi Saxena, September 2026.

## Run it

Python 3.11+, Mac or Linux. Roughly 8 minutes end to end, most of it
downloading.

**1. Clone and install** (2-4 min, torch is the big one)

    git clone https://github.com/khushi-saxena/cozmo-case-study
    cd cozmo-case-study
    python3 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
    pip install -r requirements.txt

**2. Fetch the depth model** (~30 s, 100 MB, cached after)

    bash scripts/fetch_weights.sh

**3. Fetch a capture.** There are no captures in git - they are 200-400 MB
each. Get them before running anything:

    bash scripts/fetch_captures.sh

If Google Drive's virus-scan page blocks the script, download by hand:
https://drive.google.com/file/d/16VfuW4t1hZvr9IrdqhAt4pZuEEVgnZdO/view
then `unzip -o cozmo-captures.zip -d benchmark/`

**4. Run it**

    python3 scripts/run.py --capture benchmark/captures/capture_1788930419 --tier lidar

Writes `output.json` and `plan.svg` to `benchmark/runs/`. Open the SVG in a
browser to see the plan.

**Same capture, other tiers:**

    python3 scripts/run.py --capture benchmark/captures/capture_1788930419 --tier video
    python3 scripts/run.py --capture benchmark/captures/capture_1788930419 --tier photo

**Drift ablation** (the on/off comparison in the technical report):

    python3 scripts/run.py --capture benchmark/captures/capture_1788930419 --tier lidar --no-drift-correction

**Score everything against the gates:**

    python3 benchmark/harness/gates.py

## The capture app

`ios/CozmoCapture/` - Swift and ARKit. Records RGB frames, LiDAR depth and
confidence, per-frame poses and intrinsics, plus separate stills that carry no
pose or depth by design, so the photo tier cannot cheat.

**Install:** open the xcodeproj in Xcode, set Signing -> Team to any free
Apple ID, change the bundle identifier, plug in an iPhone, Cmd-R, then trust
the developer on the phone. Under 10 minutes on a Mac that already has Xcode.

**`ios/INSTALL.md` is the full walkthrough**, including every failure I hit
while building this and how to fix each one - empty Compile Sources, stale
build cache, the 7-day certificate expiry, device pairing, keychain prompts.
Worth skimming before you start rather than after something breaks.

No TestFlight, so this assumes a Mac with Xcode. Said up front rather than
discovered later.

**Capturing a room:** `protocol/capture_protocol.md`. The one thing that
matters most: open every interior door first. A closed door reads as solid
wall to the LiDAR and will never be detected as an opening.

## Results

`capture_1788930419`: bedroom, walk-in closet, attached washroom.

| tier | rooms | floor area | ceiling height | openings | runtime |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LiDAR | 2 | 15.48 m2 | 2.430 m | 1 | 17.9 s |
| video | 2 | 13.84 m2 | 2.040 m | 0 | 29.1 s |
| photo | 2 | 10.35 m2 | 2.934 m | 0 | 40.0 s |

magicplan 2026.35.0 on the same room: ceiling 2.413 m, floor area 15.36 m2.
So 1.7 cm and 0.8% apart on the two shared dimensions.

Repeatability, two separate captures of the same room: 2.430 m both times,
0.00 cm spread.

Walk-in dry run on a room the pipeline had never seen: LiDAR ceiling within
3.4 cm of magicplan; floor area 69% low, because the room's floor is covered
in furniture and my outline comes from observed floor. Full write-up in the
technical report, section 7b.

A clean clone reproduces all three tiers to identical figures: fresh checkout,
fresh venv, weights fetched by script, 18.6 s to a LiDAR plan.

The photo tier takes per-room folders of stills with no poses and no depth and
produces two rooms with adjacency. On this capture the folders were recovered
after the fact by `scripts/split_stills.py`, because I left one room name in
the app for the whole session - see the technical report, section 8.

**Openings are the weak row** - 0 of 3 detected - and they are the subject of
the fix loop. That and every other known failure mode is in
`reports/technical_report.md` section 8. Nothing in `COMPLIANCE.csv` is marked
done that is not.

## Disclosure

One pretrained model: `depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Indoor-Small-hf`,
fetched once by `scripts/fetch_weights.sh` into the local cache. Camera focal
length (1365 px at 1920x1440) is passed as a device calibration constant at
the photo tier and stated in every photo-tier output. Nothing calls external
infrastructure at inference time.
